# A2Z Bharat

> **Experiencing the essence of India, one software at a time.**  
> An open-source initiative dedicated to capturing the essence of India while solving regional challenges including engineering localized, offline-first & cryptographically secure digital solutions for accessibility challenged states and union territories of India.

## The Chapters

A2Z Bharat develops open-source software addressing regional and civic challenges, prioritizing zero-connectivity resilience, civil protection, and ecological preservation.

### Chapter A: Arunachal Pradesh — Kangto

**Focus Area:** Mountain safety SOS, forest fire prevention, and eco-stewardship.

**Source Code:** [https://github.com/pourush5/Kangto](https://github.com/pourush5/Kangto)

**Google Play:** [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pourush.kangto](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pourush.kangto)

### Chapter B: Bihar — Saakh (साख)

**Focus Area:** Informal workforce wage theft prevention via offline Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm (ECDSA).

**Source Code:** [https://github.com/pourush5/Saakh](https://github.com/pourush5/Saakh)

**Google Play:** [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pourush.saakh](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pourush.saakh)
